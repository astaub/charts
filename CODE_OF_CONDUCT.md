# Code of Conduct 📊

`charts` is built by humans and coding agents working together. The same
standard applies to both.

## The standard

Be respectful, be precise, and assume good faith. Concretely:

- Critique the code, the render, or the idea — never the person.
- Keep issues, PRs, and reviews on topic and grounded in evidence (a repro, a
  failing test, a screenshot).
- Welcome newcomers and first-time contributors. "Here's how this works" beats
  "you should have known."
- No harassment, personal attacks, demeaning language, or unwelcome attention —
  in issues, PRs, commits, or any project space.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, commit messages,
discussions — and whenever you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that breaks this standard, report it
privately to the maintainers via the repository's
[**Report a concern**](https://github.com/astaub/charts/security/advisories/new)
form (GitHub keeps it confidential) or by opening a minimal private channel with
a maintainer. Reports are handled discreetly and in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove contributions that break this standard,
and may warn or ban contributors who do so repeatedly or egregiously.
Enforcement decisions favor keeping the project safe and welcoming over any one
contribution.
